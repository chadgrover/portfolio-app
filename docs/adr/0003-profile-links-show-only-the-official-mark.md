# Show each profile link as its official mark alone

Each profile link is shown as its company's official mark and nothing else: no visible name beside it. The mark is the white variant from the company's own download, served byte-for-byte, and the link's accessible name comes from interface text. Both companies permit their mark as a link to a profile: GitHub's logo guidelines (https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo) list "Social button: Use the Invertocat logo as a social button to link to your GitHub profile or project", and LinkedIn's (https://brand.linkedin.com/in-logo) allow it "As a hyperlink to your LinkedIn profile". Both also forbid combining the mark with words: GitHub's "combining with other words or design elements", LinkedIn's "Combine the [in] Logo with any other symbol, logo, words, images, or designs". The mark alone is the only arrangement both clearly allow.

Neither company states a minimum size or clear space for the mark alone. The portfolio borrows both from LinkedIn's rules for its full logo (https://brand.linkedin.com/linkedin-logo): the mark is at least 21px tall on screen, and a clear space of one mark height on every side holds nothing but the page's ground. Hover and focus are drawn outside that clear space.

## Considered Options

- **Mark beside its name**: easier to scan, but both companies forbid combining the mark with words, and a gap between them only argues the point rather than settling it.
- **Name only**: no brand terms to meet, but it gives up the recognition a recruiter gets from the marks at a glance.

## Consequences

A profile link carries no visible text, so its accessible name in interface text is the only place its company is named in words; keep it localized.

Adding a profile link means downloading its company's official mark, recording where it came from in `DESIGN.md`, and checking that company's guidelines against this decision before it ships.
