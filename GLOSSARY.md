# Portfolio

The portfolio presents a developer's professional information to visitors.

## Language

**Portfolio owner**:
The developer whose professional information the portfolio presents.

**Portfolio visitor**:
A person who views the portfolio, such as an engineer, technical hiring manager, or recruiter.

**Portfolio content**:
The professional information presented about the portfolio owner.
_Avoid_: Published content, public information

**Contact method**:
A way for a portfolio visitor to reach the portfolio owner directly.
_Avoid_: Contact info, contact details

**Profile link**:
A link to the portfolio owner's page on another site, such as GitHub or LinkedIn. A profile link can also serve as a contact method.
_Avoid_: Social link, social media

**Private information**:
Information that must never appear in the portfolio or its source, such as secrets, personal details the portfolio owner has not chosen to publish, or proprietary client code.
_Avoid_: Personal content

**Build output**:
The complete set of files the production build produces, which the host serves to portfolio visitors exactly as built.
_Avoid_: Built site, build artifact

**Contributor**:
A person who works on the portfolio's source through its issues and pull requests, such as the portfolio owner.
_Avoid_: Maintainer, collaborator
